Lease cheat sheet

The math, briefly

Depreciation = (Adjusted Cap Cost − Residual) ÷ Term
Rent charge = (Adjusted Cap Cost + Residual) × Money Factor
Pre-tax payment = Depreciation + Rent charge
Monthly = Pre-tax × (1 + Tax%)
APR = Money Factor × 2400

Adjusted Cap Cost = Selling Price + Acquisition Fee − Down − Trade − Rebates. Tax handling varies by state — this app applies tax to the monthly payment (most common), not on the full sale price.
